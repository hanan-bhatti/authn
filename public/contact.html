About

About Authn

Why this project exists and what drives it.

🎯 Mission

Authentication is critical infrastructure — yet most developers either roll insecure homebrew solutions or hand full control to third-party vendors. Authn exists to provide a free, self-hosted, production-grade alternative that gives you complete ownership of your auth stack without sacrificing security or developer experience.

🏗️ Tech Stack

Node.js ≥ 16 Express.js MongoDB / Mongoose JWT bcryptjs Firebase Admin SDK Sharp (image processing) Nodemailer node-cron Helmet + CORS
