TransitFLOW

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2025

What TransitFLOW Does

TransitFLOW helps you plan public transport journeys in Lahore. We show you bus routes, timings, and help you get from point A to point B using public transport.

Information We Collect

Required Information:

  • Your location (exact coordinates and address) to find nearby routes
  • Start and end destinations for trip planning
  • Email, username, and name for your account

Optional Information You Can Provide:

  • Profile picture (stored securely on AWS)
  • Phone number, date of birth, bio, website
  • Home/work locations for faster trip planning
  • App theme preference

Automatically Collected:

  • Your trip history (kept for 1 year for personalized suggestions)
  • Device information to prevent unauthorized logins
  • App usage patterns to improve the service

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

• Show you relevant bus routes and timings
• Give you personalized route recommendations
• Remember your frequently used locations
• Keep your account secure with device verification
• Improve our route prediction algorithms

Data Storage & Security

🔒 Where Your Data Lives

Profile pictures are stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Everything else (trips, preferences, account info) is stored in our MongoDB database.

We use device verification to protect your account. When you log in from a new device, you'll need to verify it's really you.

Data Sharing

🛡️ We Don't Sell Your Data

We don't share, sell, or trade your personal information with anyone. Period.

We do use:

• Google Maps for map display and location services
• Social login providers (Google, Facebook, etc.) only for account authentication

When we start working with the government for real-time data, we'll only receive their schedule information. We won't share your data with them.

Data Retention

Data Type How Long We Keep It
Trip History 1 year (for better recommendations)
Account Data Until you delete your account
Profile Pictures Deleted immediately when you remove them
Device Information Until you remove the device or delete account

Account Deletion

You can delete your account anytime in app settings. Here's what happens:

📅 Deletion Timeline

Immediately: Your profile becomes inaccessible
7 days: All your personal data is permanently deleted
30 days: Your username becomes available for others to use

Some anonymous usage statistics may be kept to help improve the app, but nothing that can identify you personally.

App Permissions

TransitFLOW requests these permissions:

• Location: Required to find nearby transport and plan routes
• Notifications: To alert you about your trips and service updates
• Additional permissions may be requested as we add new features

Your Rights

You can:

• View and edit your profile information anytime
• Turn off location sharing (app won't work properly)
• Delete individual trips from your history
• Export your data (contact us)
• Delete your entire account

Updates to This Policy

As TransitFLOW is still in development, we may update this privacy policy when we add new features. We'll notify you of important changes through the app.
